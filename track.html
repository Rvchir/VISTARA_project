Refunds and resales

If a fixture is cancelled outright, we refund the full amount including the booking fee, within five working days.

If a fixture moves date or kick-off time, your seats move with it. Tell us within seven days if the new date does not work and we will refund you.

Change of mind is not refundable, but you can list your seats for resale up to four hours before kick-off.
